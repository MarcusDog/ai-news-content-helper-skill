# Content formats

## Short video

Use a 45–90 second structure: audience pain point, verified change, two or three evidence-backed implications, one caution, and a low-cost action. Keep citations in the manuscript or provide a fact-check sheet immediately below it.

## Article

Use: problem, conclusion preview, evidence by perspective, counterpoint/limitations, practical actions, evidence boundary, and sources. Each section must answer “how does this help the intended reader?”

## Newsletter

Use: this week's judgment, three signals, domestic/international comparison, what changed from the prior period, next-week watchlist, and sources.

## Xiaohongshu

Use: specific pain-point hook, 3–5 findings, mistakes to avoid, a saveable checklist, and sources. Do not exaggerate certainty for engagement.

## Practical answer

Use: direct answer, assumptions, evidence-backed options, decision rule, smallest safe experiment, risks, and sources. Avoid prescribing expensive actions without a verification step.
